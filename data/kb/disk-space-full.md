# Disk space full
Category: Hardware
Keywords: disk full, storage full, c drive
Severity: Low

## Symptoms
Low disk space warning or failed saves.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Empty the Recycle Bin.
2. Run Disk Cleanup.
3. Uninstall unused programs.
4. Move large files to cloud storage.

## Permitted Actions
- run_cleanup_script(user_id)
- create_ticket(category="Hardware")

## When to Escalate
- Space is still low after cleanup.
