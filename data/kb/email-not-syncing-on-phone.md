# Email not syncing on phone
Category: Email
Keywords: phone mail, sync, mobile email
Severity: Low

## Symptoms
New mails do not appear on the phone.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check mobile data or Wi-Fi.
2. Confirm sync is turned on for mail.
3. Remove and add the account again.
4. Update the mail app.

## Permitted Actions
- reenable_mail_sync(user_id)
- create_ticket(category="Email")

## When to Escalate
- Password was changed recently and sync still fails.
