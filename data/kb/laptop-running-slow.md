# Laptop running slow
Category: Hardware
Keywords: slow, laptop slow, hang, romba slow
Severity: Low

## Symptoms
Apps open slowly and the fan is loud.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Close unused apps and browser tabs.
2. End high-CPU tasks in Task Manager.
3. Free disk space until usage is below 85%.
4. Restart and install pending updates.

## Permitted Actions
- run_cleanup_script(user_id)
- create_ticket(category="Hardware")

## When to Escalate
- Still slow even after cleanup.
