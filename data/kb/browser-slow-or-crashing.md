# Browser slow or crashing
Category: Software
Keywords: browser, chrome, slow browser, crash
Severity: Low

## Symptoms
Pages load slowly or the browser closes.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Close extra tabs.
2. Clear cache and cookies.
3. Disable extensions.
4. Update or reinstall the browser.

## Permitted Actions
- clear_browser_data(user_id)
- create_ticket(category="Software")

## When to Escalate
- The problem happens in every browser.
