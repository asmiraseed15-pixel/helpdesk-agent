# No audio or microphone in meetings
Category: Software
Keywords: mic, no sound, teams audio, zoom audio
Severity: Low

## Symptoms
Others cannot hear you or you cannot hear them.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Select the right mic and speaker in the meeting app.
2. Check Windows sound settings and unmute.
3. Allow microphone access in Privacy settings.
4. Restart the app or reconnect the headset.

## Permitted Actions
- run_audio_check(device_id)
- create_ticket(category="Software")

## When to Escalate
- The audio device is not detected at all.
