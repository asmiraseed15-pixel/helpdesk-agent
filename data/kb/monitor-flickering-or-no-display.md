# Monitor flickering or no display
Category: Hardware
Keywords: monitor, display, screen flicker, no signal
Severity: Low

## Symptoms
Screen flickers or shows no signal.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check cable connections at both ends.
2. Try another cable or port.
3. Lower the refresh rate in display settings.
4. Update the graphics driver.

## Permitted Actions
- open_driver_update(device_id)
- create_ticket(category="Hardware")

## When to Escalate
- Flicker continues on another monitor.
