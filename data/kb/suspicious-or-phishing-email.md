# Suspicious or phishing email
Category: Security
Keywords: phishing, suspicious email, spam, scam
Severity: High

## Symptoms
Email asks for passwords or contains odd links.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Do not click links or open attachments.
2. Report it using the Report Phishing button.
3. Delete the email.
4. If you clicked, change your password immediately.

## Permitted Actions
- quarantine_email(message_id)
- create_ticket(category="Security")

## When to Escalate
- User entered credentials or opened a file.
