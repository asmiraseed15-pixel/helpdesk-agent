# Outlook Not Opening

Category: Software
Severity: Medium
Keywords: outlook, email, not opening, crash, not responding

## Symptoms
- Outlook freezes on the loading screen.
- Outlook closes immediately after opening.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Close Outlook fully from Task Manager and reopen it.
2. Start Outlook in safe mode (hold Ctrl while opening).
3. Disable add-ins one by one.
4. Check that there is enough free disk space.
5. Create a new Outlook profile from Control Panel > Mail.
6. Repair Microsoft Office from Settings > Apps.

## Permitted Actions
- create_ticket(category="Software")

## When to Escalate
- Mailbox data is corrupted or emails are missing.
- Outlook still fails after a new profile.
