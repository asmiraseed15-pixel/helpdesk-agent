# Keyboard or mouse not working
Category: Hardware
Keywords: keyboard, mouse, not working, usb
Severity: Low

## Symptoms
Keys or cursor do not respond.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Replace batteries or recharge wireless devices.
2. Unplug and reconnect, or try another USB port.
3. Restart the laptop.
4. Test on another computer.

## Permitted Actions
- create_replacement_request(user_id, device)
- create_ticket(category="Hardware")

## When to Escalate
- The built-in laptop keyboard fails.
