# Outlook not opening
Category: Email
Keywords: outlook, mail open aagala, email
Severity: Medium

## Symptoms
Outlook crashes or stays on the loading screen.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Start Outlook in safe mode.
2. Disable add-ins one by one.
3. Repair the Office installation.
4. Create a new mail profile.

## Permitted Actions
- repair_office(user_id)
- create_ticket(category="Email")

## When to Escalate
- Mailbox data is corrupted or emails are missing.
- Outlook still fails after a new profile.
